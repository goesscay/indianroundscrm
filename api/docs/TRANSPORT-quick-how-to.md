# Transport: one-page how-to (Operations and Sales)

## Operations: keep the lists (Operations → Transport)
| Tab | What it holds | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Rates** | Road transport (cab, van, bus, ferry) with prices. One line per rate. | Operations only |
| **Flights** | Flight details: route, times, days, class, note. **No price.** | Everyone (as suggestions) |
| **Trains** | Train details, same as flights. **No price.** | Everyone (as suggestions) |

1. **Rates:** quick add a line (type, destination, service, price, how it is charged) or **Import from sheet** (Check first, then Import). Optional: supplier, season dates, seats, hours. Seasons sit inside the line (⋯ → Add season). Several rates can change by a percentage (tick them → Change prices → Preview).
2. **Flights and Trains:** **Add** or **Import from Excel**. Tick the days it runs. If a service ends or starts *near* a destination, write that destination in **Also serves** and say how to continue in the **note**.
3. **Keep it fresh:** a service not touched for 180 days shows **Needs update**. Open it and save once you have checked it.

## Sales: the Transport card (Quotes → New premium quotation)
1. Fill in the trip first (destination, **travel date**, nights, travellers).
2. **Road transport tab** (priced): pick **Same vehicle every day** or **Choose day by day**. Use the buttons: **Add another car**, **Add another destination**, **Add Volvo Bus**, **Add Ferry** (and **Add train**, coming). The bar at the bottom shows the **Transport price**.
3. **Flights and Trains tabs** (no price): the list is filtered by route and date. Use **Going** for the way there and **Return** for the way back (enter **From** first). **Tick** what to show, or add one just for this quote.
4. **Rate needed** means no rate covers that date: ask Operations. You cannot type a price over it.
5. Check the **package price**: ticking flights and trains never changes it.

## Check before sending
- No "Rate needed" warning on the card.
- Open the PDF: the **Your transport** page and, if you ticked any, **How to reach**.
- The customer is told flights and trains are suggestions: timings are confirmed at booking.

## Who to ask
- A missing or wrong rate, flight or train: Operations.
- The wording the customer sees: car names show and the transport total includes GST. Quotations saved earlier keep their old choice.
