# Transport in a Premium Quotation: guide for Sales

**Where:** Quotes → New premium quotation → the **Transport** card (under Hotel options). One card, three tabs, each with a count: **Road transport**, **Flights** and **Trains**. Road transport is priced from the rates Operations keep. Flights and trains show details only, with no price.

At the bottom of the card a bar always shows the **Transport price**. It counts road transport only, and updates as you change things.

## Road transport

1. **Set the trip first.** Destination, travel date, nights and travellers. The rate is found from the travel date and season, so set the date before choosing.
2. **Cab for the stay** has two buttons:
   - **Same vehicle every day** (the default): choose the **Car name**, then the **Route service** (it stays locked until a car is chosen, and is filled in for you when the car has only one route). The number of days comes from the itinerary (change it if needed). The rate per day and the amount appear at once, and the price follows if you change the vehicle.
   - **Choose day by day:** one line per day with that day's plan beside it. Pick the **Car name** and then the **Route service** for each day, or **No car** (for example the arrival day by Volvo).
   Switching between the two keeps everything you added below.
3. **The five buttons under the card:** **Add another car** (a second car for the same days), **Add another destination**, **Add Volvo Bus**, **Add Ferry** and **Add train** (coming). A one-off transfer, such as an airport pickup, is a route service charged per trip: pick it in a car row and it is priced once. Choose the car (or bus or boat) name, then its route service, the day, and one way or return. The number of vehicles is worked out for you. A bus counts the travellers, and a return counts twice.
4. **The number of vehicles** is worked out from the travellers and the seats, and shown under the row (for example "2 vehicles for 8 guests, 6 seats each"). For a second, different car on the same days press **Add another car**: it counts as one vehicle.
5. **"Rate needed"** means no rate covers that date. It is left out of the price. Ask Operations to add or fix the rate; you cannot type a price over it.
6. **A trip with several destinations:** use **Add another destination** under the same-vehicle choice, then pick that destination's vehicle and the day it starts.
7. **Foreign prices** (for example Singapore): converted at the exchange rate held on the quotation, which Operations keep as a default. If it is missing the card says so.
8. **What the customer sees:** car names show ("Private Ertiga") and the transport total shows including GST. The box that changed these on the card is gone; quotations saved earlier keep the choice they had.

You see selling prices only. Suppliers, costs and margin are never shown to you or to the customer.

## Flights and trains (suggestions, no price)

Open the **Flights** or **Trains** tab. Details only: nothing is priced and the package price never changes.

1. The list is already filtered by the trip: **From** (where the customer starts), **To** (the destination) and the **travel date**. Only services that run on that weekday are shown. A service that ends near the destination (for example a train to Kalka for Manali) is shown with its note on how to continue.
2. **Going / Return:** *Going* lists services from where the customer starts to the destination. Switch to *Return* for the way back: the date becomes the last day of the trip (change it if needed) and the list shows services that start at or near the destination and end where the customer started. Enter **From** first: the way back needs to know where it is going back to.
3. **Tick** the ones to share with the customer. Going and return ticks are kept together and the tab count shows the total.
4. Not on the list? **Add a flight or train typed for this quote only** (it is added for the direction you are on). It is kept on this quotation and is not added to the master list.
5. The customer sees a **How to reach** block with the details, the running days (return services are marked **Return**, after the ones for the way there) and a note that these are suggestions with no price and that timings must be confirmed at booking.

## Older quotations

A quotation saved before the Transport card opens as it was and prices exactly the same. If it has transport from the earlier layout, it is shown under the card and still works.

**When something is missing:** if the destination has no cab or van in the rate list, the card says so and tells you Operations add one. If a car has no price for the travel date, the row says **Rate needed**, the amount shows a dash, and the Transport price bar warns that the price is not complete. Changing the car clears the route service (unless the new car has only one route). A quotation saved before the Car name and Route service dropdowns opens with the same prices.
