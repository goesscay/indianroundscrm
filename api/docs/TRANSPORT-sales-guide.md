# Transport in a Premium Quotation: guide for Sales

**Where:** Quotes → New premium quotation → the **Transport** section (under Hotel options).

1. **Set the trip first.** Destination, travel date, nights and travellers. The transport rates are found from the travel date, so set it before adding transport.
2. **Add a leg** with the buttons for each option: Bus, Cab, Tempo / van, Ferry. Each option (1, 2, 3) can have different transport.
3. **Flights and trains** are not priced legs. They are suggestions in their own section (see below).
4. **Bus, cab, van, ferry:** choose the **service**, the **vehicle** and the **route**. The price comes from the transport master. The number of vehicles is suggested from the group size ("auto"); type a number to change it. For a bus choose one way or return.
5. **Cabs:** under "Cabs in <destination>" choose *Same cab for the whole stay* (one cab, set the number of days) or *Cab day by day* (add a cab for each day, each can be different).
6. **"Rate needed"** means no rate covers that date. The leg is left out of the price. Ask Operations to add or fix the rate; never type a price over it.
7. **"In the price"** unticked shows the leg to the customer for information, without adding it to the total.
8. **Exchange rates:** if a leg is in a foreign currency, type today's rate (₹ per 1 unit). It is saved on the quotation, so the price does not change when it is reopened.
9. **What the customer sees:** choose to show vehicle and airline names ("Private Ertiga") or only a general description ("Private cab"), and whether the transport total shows with GST or before GST.

You see selling prices only. Vendor names, costs and margin are never shown to you or to the customer.

The customer's PDF and the quote page show a **Your transport** section with a line for each leg, a total, and the notes that apply. The transport inclusions are added to the Inclusions list automatically.


## Flights and trains (suggestions, no price)

**Where:** the **Flights and trains** section, under Transport. Details only: nothing is priced and the package price never changes.

1. The list is already filtered by the trip: **From** (where the customer starts), **To** (the destination) and the **travel date**. Only services that run on that weekday are shown. A service that ends near the destination (for example a train to Kalka for Manali) is shown with its note on how to continue.
2. **Tick** the ones to share with the customer. "Show" switches between flights and trains.
3. Not on the list? **Add a flight or train typed for this quote only.** It is kept on this quotation and is not added to the master list.
4. The customer sees a **How to reach** block with the details, the running days and a note that these are suggestions with no price and that timings must be confirmed at booking.
